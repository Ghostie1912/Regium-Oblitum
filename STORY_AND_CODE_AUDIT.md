# REGIUM OBLITUM — Reimagined Edition: Story and Build Audit

## Source and narrative boundaries

Based on the author's Chapters 1–11 and 13–19, shared in conversation. Chapter 12 has **not** been supplied. The script marks the transition from 11 to 13 as an omitted chapter instead of inventing its events. Chapters 1–10 follow the earlier playable adaptation, with Chapter 10 continued according to the newly shared text. Dialogues in the later chapters are selectively condensed for pacing, with the main named events intact.

## Visual and interaction changes in this edition

- Redesigned atmospheric title, controls, pause, chapter splash and dialogue UI.
- New procedural painted scenes (birch woodland, flower valley, dead bird and parchment, body discovery, creature reveal, epilogue).
- Contextual particle, light, fog and tint overlays.
- Ambient sound bed and new small cue sounds with mute control.
- Chapter select to access representative scenes, and saved major chapter checkpoints when local storage works.
- Easy spider chase: auto-run, no rock obstacles, occasional projectile hazard, lenient hits.
- Original uploaded video preserved as cinematic with skip and mute.

## Meaningful limits

This remains a hand-coded **2D** canvas/pixel-art browser game; it is not a 3D-modelled game. Complex multi-ending design remains unwritten and is not promised. The story adaptation is shorter than the original text and is not intended to replace the author's manuscript.

## QA

The JavaScript passes Node syntax verification and the story-entry / Canvas mock test. Automated Chromium navigation was blocked by environment policy and the full game was not visually playtested across every branch. Full browser testing by the user is strongly recommended.
