REGIUM OBLITUM — THE REIMAGINED EDITION
========================================

HOW TO PLAY
-----------
1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder. Do not move index.html by itself.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
3. Choose BEGIN ADVENTURE to watch the author-supplied opening film, then play the expedition.
4. Choose SKIP CINEMATIC to enter the guild immediately.
5. Use CHAPTER SELECT to revisit major story chapters (including the new forest and ending).
6. CONTINUE FROM CHECKPOINT is enabled after you begin a major chapter. It relies on the browser's local storage and may be unavailable in some restrictive private/file browsing modes.

CONTROLS
--------
WASD / Arrow keys    Move in rooms, tunnels, forest and boat
E / Enter             Interact or advance dialogue
Mouse click           Advance dialogue
Space                 Jump over occasional spider web projectiles
1–9                   Choose dialogue options when visible
Esc                   Pause / resume
M                     Toggle atmosphere sounds
F                     Fullscreen (if browser permits)

THE GAME
--------
- Fully refreshed cinematic interface: title screen, legible book-style dialogue,
  environmental light, textured frame, chapter cards, improved scene-specific grading.
- Author's original MP4 opening cinematic is embedded; original audio is preserved.
- Four explorable guild rooms, with people and clickable props.
- Cavern footprints, huge maze, interactions, reunion and burial scenes.
- Forgiving automated spider pursuit. No rock obstacles. Rare web projectiles.
- Welt's visualized memories, two-boat sea reveal, playable constellation-sea boat,
  memories in the water, and Sir Neighs-a-Lot.
- New second half: painted birch forest, Silas's dream and flower valley, parchment,
  Aaron's death, the shapeshifting creature, escape and epilogue 20 years later.
- Chapter select and saved major-chapter checkpoints, as supported by browser.

STORY ACCURACY
--------------
The author's manuscript for Chapters I–XI and XIII–XIX was used as the basis. The
spoken lines for later chapters are condensed and lightly edited to fit interactive
pacing; this is an adaptation, not a verbatim book edition.

CHAPTER XII was not supplied. The game explicitly marks the narrative gap and moves
from Chapter XI to XIII. No missing events are invented.

Velvid, NOT a different woman, is the mother in Chapter XIX. The epilogue takes
place 20 years after the expedition. The opposing chapter titles are a narrative
parallel, NOT a literal 100-year gap.

This is a handcrafted browser-based 2D pixel / illustrated game, not a 3D engine.
The authored video is provided only as the opening cinematic.
