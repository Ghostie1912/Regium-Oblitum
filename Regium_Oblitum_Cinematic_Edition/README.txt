REGIUM OBLITUM — CINEMATIC EDITION
=================================
A playable ten-chapter pixel-art fantasy adventure with your supplied opening film.

START
1. Extract the complete ZIP (keep all files together).
2. Open index.html with Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. Click BEGIN ADVENTURE to watch your uploaded 73-second opening video (with its sound).
4. The opening automatically flows into Chapter I — the playable Explorers' Guild.
5. SKIP INTRO skips only the movie. The title screen also has a skip-to-guild button.

Your original video (1104(2).mp4) is included as assets/intro_cinematic.mp4,
compressed to a web-friendly 720p H.264 MP4 without intentionally changing the edit or audio.
No computer-generated narration replaces your trailer audio.

KEYS
WASD / Arrows  Move / steer boat
E / Enter      Interact / continue dialogue (first tap completes typing, second advances)
Click dialogue  Same as Enter
1–9            Select presented story choice
Space          Jump over the occasional glowing spider web attack
D / Right      Speed up automatically running character during chase
Escape         Pause / resume the game
M              Toggle synthesized low-volume atmospheric background tones

WHAT'S BEEN IMPROVED
- Full-screen opening movie with playback progress and audio/skip controls.
- Redesigned title screen, dialogue boxes, menus and in-game HUD.
- Cinematic chapter reveals, subtler lighting, drifting atmospheric motes and
  scene-specific finishing passes for caves, flower fields and underground sea.
- Typed dialogue with the option to reveal immediately, plus responsive controls.
- Pause/resume interface; quick return to title via pause menu.
- Gentle ambient audio variation for the guild, caves, chase, flower field and sea;
  this starts only from player interaction and never plays over the intro movie.
- Previous easy spider chase RETAINED: no rocks, automatic running, spaced-out
  glowing web shots that only slow the character on impact.

STORY
Ten chapters follow the existing manuscript and previous Director's Cut:
Guild, Cave, Lost Ways, Maze Runner, Lost Team, Ashlyn, Exit / Welt memory,
Sea of Constellation, visions, narwhal and the manuscript's boat-stop cliffhanger.
The author has not yet written the larger promised endings, so those aren't invented.

IMPORTANT
This is an improved browser-based pixel-art adventure, NOT a newly rebuilt 3D game.
Most environments are hand-painted with JavaScript canvas and sprites.
It preserves the current game mechanics and story. There is no save system.
Some browsers require pressing play on the video's built-in controls if autoplay
following a click is blocked. The cinematic also has a SKIP button.
